## Elevate Your Lexical Resource with Academic Collocations

To achieve a high band score in IELTS Writing Task 2, candidates must demonstrate a strong command of vocabulary. In IELTS Academic, Writing Task 2 requires an essay of at least 250 words within a total 60-minute writing section (which also includes Task 1, requiring at least 150 words). Writing and Speaking are each scored on four criteria, one of which is Lexical Resource. Lexical Resource evaluates your range, precision, collocation, word formation, and spelling. Using advanced vocabulary in natural, precise collocations is essential for demonstrating academic English proficiency.

Mastering collocations means learning which words naturally go together in formal academic contexts. Instead of relying on generic phrases or repeating common words, using precise adjectives and verbs helps you construct nuanced arguments. For example, describing an approach as [meticulous](/words/meticulous) or an attitude as [intransigent](/words/intransigent) allows you to convey complex ideas efficiently. Understanding how to pair advanced words with common academic nouns (such as *research*, *policy*, or *demographics*) will make your writing clearer, more formal, and more persuasive.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1
**Question:** *Some people believe that governments should spend public money on major infrastructure projects, while others argue that state funds should be managed conservatively to avoid national debt. Discuss both views and give your opinion.*  

**Sample Response Excerpt:**  
Proponents of fiscal restraint argue that governments must remain [frugal](/words/frugal) when allocating taxpayer money. Rather than committing vast sums to unproven ventures, state planners should carefully [scrutinize](/words/scrutinize) every proposed expenditure to ensure long-term public benefit. When managing public finances, policymakers need to be exceptionally [circumspect](/words/circumspect), weighing immediate economic stimulus against the threat of severe long-term national debt.

**Analysis:**  
This response uses high-level vocabulary accurately within an economic context. The phrase "remain frugal" forms a strong collocation indicating careful financial management. Combining "scrutinize" with "expenditure" demonstrates precision, while describing policymakers as "circumspect" accurately reflects the cautious approach required in fiscal planning. These choices fulfill the criteria for high precision and appropriate word formation under Lexical Resource.

### Example 2
**Question:** *In many modern societies, traditional cultural practices are rapidly changing due to globalization. Is this a positive or negative development?*  

**Sample Response Excerpt:**  
Globalization has transformed modern populations into a deeply [heterogeneous](/words/heterogeneous) mix of cultural influences. While some traditionalists are [tenacious](/words/tenacious) in their efforts to preserve legacy customs, others argue that communities should not remain completely [intransigent](/words/intransigent) in the face of modern social progress. A balanced perspective requires a [magnanimous](/words/magnanimous) stance that values diversity while respecting historical heritage.

**Analysis:**  
Here, the word "heterogeneous" forms a precise academic collocation with "mix of cultural influences" (or *heterogeneous society*). Describing traditionalists as "tenacious" and their posture as potentially "intransigent" provides clear contrast between persistence and unyielding rigidity. Finally, using "magnanimous" to describe an open-minded approach toward cultural integration adds sophistication and range to the essay's vocabulary.

### Example 3
**Question:** *Some argue that intense workplace competition improves productivity, while others believe it causes unnecessary stress among employees. Discuss both views and give your opinion.*  

**Sample Response Excerpt:**  
Excessive workplace pressure can severely [enervate](/words/enervate) staff members, leading to widespread burnout and diminished overall output. To maintain a healthy work environment, corporate leaders ought to encourage employees to [abstain](/words/abstain) from working excessive uncompensated overtime. Sustainable productivity relies on supportive management practices rather than high-stress environments.

**Analysis:**  
Using "enervate" to describe the effect of pressure on staff provides a more precise verb than common alternatives like *weaken* or *tire*. Furthermore, collocator phrases such as "abstain from working excessive... overtime" demonstrate correct prepositional use and accurate collocation, directly satisfying the requirements for top-band Lexical Resource.

## Words to Know for IELTS

- **[frugal](/words/frugal)**: Economical in the use of resources, especially money or food.  
  *Collocations:* frugal expenditure, frugal mindset, remain frugal.
- **[tenacious](/words/tenacious)**: Holding firmly to an opinion, goal, or object; persistent.  
  *Collocations:* tenacious effort, tenacious hold, tenacious defender.
- **[abstain](/words/abstain)**: To formally refrain from an action or practice.  
  *Collocations:* abstain from voting, abstain from consuming, choose to abstain.
- **[heterogeneous](/words/heterogeneous)**: Consisting of diverse, dissimilar constituents.  
  *Collocations:* heterogeneous population, heterogeneous group, heterogeneous culture.
- **[meticulous](/words/meticulous)**: Showing extreme care and attention to detail.  
  *Collocations:* meticulous research, meticulous planning, meticulous approach.
- **[scrutinize](/words/scrutinize)**: To examine or inspect closely and thoroughly.  
  *Collocations:* scrutinize evidence, scrutinize proposals, closely scrutinize.
- **[circumspect](/words/circumspect)**: Wary, cautious, considering all consequences.  
  *Collocations:* circumspect approach, remain circumspect, circumspect attitude.
- **[intransigent](/words/intransigent)**: Refusing to compromise; uncompromisingly stubborn.  
  *Collocations:* intransigent position, intransigent view, remain intransigent.
- **[magnanimous](/words/magnanimous)**: Generous or forgiving toward a rival or less powerful person.  
  *Collocations:* magnanimous gesture, magnanimous decision, remain magnanimous.
- **[enervate](/words/enervate)**: To cause someone to feel drained of energy or vitality.  
  *Collocations:* enervate the workforce, enervating climate, enervating pressure.

## 5-Minute Daily Practice Routine

1. **Minute 1: Review Collocations** — Select two words from the list above and read their standard academic collocations aloud.
2. **Minute 2: Sentence Construction** — Write one IELTS Task 2 body paragraph sentence combining an advanced adjective with a noun (e.g., *meticulous research* or *heterogeneous demographics*).
3. **Minute 3: Synonym Swap** — Take a basic sentence from your past writing (e.g., "The government must check the plan carefully") and upgrade it using academic vocabulary (e.g., "The government must closely scrutinize the proposal").
4. **Minutes 4–5: Self-Correction** — Check your written sentences for correct prepositions, word forms, and spelling to ensure accuracy under Lexical Resource criteria.
