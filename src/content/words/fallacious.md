### Critical Reasoning Nuance (GMAT, GRE, TOEFL)
Standardized tests constantly evaluate logical fallacies (*ad hominem*, false causality, post hoc ergo propter hoc). When an author's argument conflates correlation with causation, answer choices routinely point out that the reasoning is **fallacious**. Pair it with synonyms like *specious* (looks right at first glance, but fundamentally false).
