### High-Yield Exam Context
Standardized tests constantly contrast *pragmatic* with *quixotic* (impractically idealistic), *visionary*, or *doctrinaire*. When a passage introduces an ambitious theoretical framework followed by budget constraints or political gridlock, look for *pragmatic* as the bridge word to the resolution.
