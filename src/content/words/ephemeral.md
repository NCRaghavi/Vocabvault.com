### Scholarly Nuance & GRE/SAT Application
In GRE Text Completion and Sentence Equivalence items, **ephemeral** frequently contrasts with words denoting durability such as *perennial*, *inveterate*, or *immutable*. International students should note that while *transient* often describes physical stays or mechanical conditions, *ephemeral* carries an aesthetic or philosophical undertone regarding the fleeting nature of time and human achievement.
