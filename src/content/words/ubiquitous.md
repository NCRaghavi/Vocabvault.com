### Academic Writing Strategy
For IELTS Academic Writing Task 2 and GRE Issue Essays, **ubiquitous** is a premier substitute for overused phrases like *'present all over the world'* or *'very common'*. Using *ubiquitous* signals lexical sophistication and precision when discussing modern technological, economic, or environmental realities.
