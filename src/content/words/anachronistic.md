### Test-Prep Breakdown
In standardized reading comprehension tests (SAT and GRE), questions frequently hinge on authors describing traditional institutions as *anachronistic* to argue that policy reform is overdue. Be cautious not to confuse *anachronistic* with *anarchic* (lawless) or *antediluvian* (extremely primitive).
