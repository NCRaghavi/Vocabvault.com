### Standardized Exam Analysis (GMAT, PTE, GRE & TOEFL)
In GMAT Critical Reasoning and PTE Reading & Writing Fill in the Blanks, **substantiate** is the premier scholarly term when an author is challenged to furnish data. In GRE Sentence Equivalence, look for pairings with *corroborate* or *authenticate*. Conversely, weak arguments that fail to substantiate conclusions are labeled as *specious* or *untenable*.
